# Data

## Data Source

This project uses microdata from the **Spanish ICT Household Survey 2025** (*Encuesta sobre Equipamiento y Uso de Tecnologías de la Información y Comunicación en los Hogares*), published by the **National Statistics Institute of Spain (INE)**.

The original dataset is not redistributed in this repository.

Researchers can obtain the official data directly from the INE website:

https://www.ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/TICH2025.htm

---

## Dataset Description

The survey provides information on:

- Household access to information and communication technologies (ICT).
- Internet use and digital skills.
- Online purchasing behaviour.
- Socioeconomic and demographic characteristics.
- Regional differences in technology adoption.

The present study focuses on the Andalusian subsample of the survey and examines the socioeconomic, demographic, and technological factors associated with advanced digital capital.
